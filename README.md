# CivicOS

**Evidence-to-action infrastructure for ordinary people, investigators, and public institutions.**

> Given what is known right now, what is the most useful thing I can do next — and why?

CivicOS connects official sources, entities, rules, evidence, public money, responsibilities, uncertainty, and safe next actions through one inspectable graph.

## Flagship verticals

1. **Benefits Graph** — discover support worth checking, show why, identify missing evidence, and route to the official next step.
2. **Public Money Graph** — follow budget → procurement → award → organisation → audit context without turning patterns into accusations.
3. **Decision Review** — inspect an administrative decision, extract facts and deadlines, verify cited rules, expose evidence gaps, and prepare a reviewable next action.

## Product contract

```text
question / document / event
    ↓
official sources + case intake
    ↓
entities + relationships
    ↓
rules + responsibility
    ↓
claims + evidence + contradictions
    ↓
current constraint
    ↓
best next action + Why?
    ↓
freshness / privacy / policy gates
    ↓
human approval where consequential
    ↓
audit + evaluation + replay
```

**The model may interpret and propose. Authority remains outside the model.**

This repository is the product shell. Existing projects such as SafeTrace, GitLaw, Public Money MCP, Citizen Agents, PrüfPilot, Judge MCP, and Digital Worker Factory remain independently testable providers behind explicit contracts.
