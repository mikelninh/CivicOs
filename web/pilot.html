CivicOS / Invite-only first tester pilot

Test the next action — not blind trust.

CivicOS is an evidence-to-action prototype. It can help you inspect benefits, public-money evidence and administrative decisions, while showing uncertainty and why it recommends a next step.

Before you enter
✓ Experimental and bounded.
This is a first-user pilot, not a government service, entitlement decision, lawyer, court, or autonomous investigator.
✓ No automatic consequential action.
CivicOS may prepare or recommend. It does not submit applications, objections, accusations or external actions for you.
✓ Personal decision documents are processed in memory.
The CivicOS application hashes the upload and does not persist the original document bytes. Use a redacted copy whenever possible.
Please do not upload high-risk secrets.
No passwords, authentication codes, bank credentials, medical records, criminal-case material, intimate information, or identity-document scans. Remove case/account numbers and unnecessary names where possible.
Adults only for this pilot.
Household scenarios may include children's ages, but do not enter children's names or sensitive personal details.
Your invite

Access lasts up to 8 hours in a signed HttpOnly session cookie. In deployed pilot mode cookies are Secure + SameSite=Strict. The CivicOS app does not intentionally log request bodies; hosting infrastructure may still process connection/operational metadata under its own configuration.

What we want you to test

Can you understand the evidence, spot uncertainty, and know what to do next?

Start with the synthetic examples first. If those make sense, try a redacted real situation. If CivicOS is confusing, wrong, overconfident or unsafe, that is exactly the feedback we need.
